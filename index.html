About Us


Cody Hub is a place where people learn to code by building real things. We offer practical tutorials, project walkthroughs, and clear explanations of languages like Python, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS, all written in plain language for beginners and experienced developers alike.

The site was founded by John Sam, an educator who saw how many talented people gave up on coding because the resources felt confusing or out of touch. John Sam set out to create something different: lessons that focus on understanding, not memorization.

"I started this site because coding should be something anyone can learn, not just a select few. My goal is to turn curiosity into confidence, one project at a time." — John Sam, Founder & CEO

Every tutorial is tested for accuracy and designed to take you from idea to working code, one step at a time. We're also a growing community of learners and builders who share ideas, ask questions, and support each other.

Whether you're writing your first line of code or leveling up your craft, you're welcome here.
